---

## 💡 Craving Survival Tips

- Drink 1 full glass of water + pinch of salt. Wait 10 mins. Craving usually gone.
- Squeeze calamansi into cold water (no sugar). Sip slowly.
- Brush your teeth — mint signals 'eating is done' to your brain.
- Do 20 squats or walk around the block. Cravings pass in 10–15 mins.
- It's habit, not real hunger. Your heavy dinner is working. Trust it.

> **Breaks the fast:** Any food ❌  |  Juice ❌  |  Coffee with sugar/creamer ❌  |  Softdrinks ❌

> Your sinigang or adobo at 7PM fills your stomach through 10–11PM.
